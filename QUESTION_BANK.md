# Question Paper Bank & Bloom's Taxonomy Subsystem

## 1. Classification Matrix
- **Difficulty Levels**: `EASY`, `MEDIUM`, `HARD`.
- **Bloom's Taxonomy Levels**: `REMEMBER`, `UNDERSTAND`, `APPLY`, `ANALYZE`, `EVALUATE`, `CREATE`.

---

## 2. Multi-Version Paper Generator
Generates randomized Version A, Version B, and Version C exam paper sets from the question bank to prevent examination room copying.
