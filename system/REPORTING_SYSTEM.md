# Institutional ERP Suite — Financial BI Reporting & Data Export

## 1. Reporting Capabilities
The BI Reporting Engine produces real-time reports:
- **Daily Collection Report**: Hour-by-hour payment reconciliation breakdown.
- **Department-wise Revenue Ledger**: Fee revenue categorized by Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil, etc.
- **Scholarship Disbursement Summary**: Total aid granted vs budget allocation.
- **Defaulters & Outstanding Report**: Filterable list of students with overdue fees.

---

## 2. Multi-Format Export Pipeline
Reports can be exported in:
- **CSV Format**: Structured tabular format for spreadsheets.
- **Excel (.xlsx)**: Formatted workbooks with department sheets.
- **PDF**: Print-ready document reports featuring institutional headers.
