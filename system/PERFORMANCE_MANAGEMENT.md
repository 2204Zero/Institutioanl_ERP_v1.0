# Institutional ERP Suite — Faculty Performance Appraisals & Research Scores

## 1. Academic Performance Indicator (API) Score Formulation

$$\text{Total Score} = \text{Teaching Feedback (40\%)} + \text{Research & Publications (40\%)} + \text{Patents & Grants (20\%)}$$

---

## 2. Research Metrics Tracked
- **Journal Publications**: Indexed in Scopus / Web of Science (15 pts / paper).
- **Patents Granted / Filed**: International and national patents (25 pts / patent).
- **Student Evaluation Feedback**: Normalized score out of 5.0 derived from 12k+ student reviews.
