# Institutional ERP Suite — Hostel & Mess Administration System

## 1. Subsystem Scope
The Hostel Management System (`src/pages/HostelPage.tsx`) manages campus residential blocks, room inventory, warden gate logs, and mess fee tracking.

---

## 2. Occupancy & Allocation Matrix
- **Room Types**: 1-Seater, 2-Seater, and 3-Seater layouts.
- **Vacancy Tracking**: Real-time bed availability updates when a student checks in/out.
- **Gate Pass Workflow**: Digital QR Gate Passes for weekend outstation permissions with automated parent SMS notifications.
