# Institutional ERP Suite — Curriculum Implementation Guide (NEP 2020 & CBCS)

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
This guide details the implementation of Choice-Based Credit System (CBCS) and National Education Policy (NEP 2020) requirements within the Institutional ERP Suite.

It guides academic administrators in setting up degree programs, multi-disciplinary baskets, credit caps, and exit options.

---

## 2. Multi-Disciplinary Credit Structure

```
Total Required Degree Credits (160 Credits for 4-Year B.Tech / B.S. Hons)
 ├── Major Core Discipline: 50% (80 Credits)
 ├── Minor / Specialization: 20% (32 Credits)
 ├── Multidisciplinary Electives: 10% (16 Credits)
 ├── Ability Enhancement (AEC) & Languages: 8% (12 Credits)
 ├── Skill Enhancement (SEC): 7% (12 Credits)
 └── Value Added (VAC) & Internship/Research: 5% (8 Credits)
```

---

## 3. Multiple Entry-Exit Architecture (NEP Compliance)

| Exit Point | Award / Certification | Min Credits Completed | Skill Certifications Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Year 1 (Sem II)** | Certificate in Discipline | 40 Credits + 4 Internship Credits | Industry Foundation Badge |
| **Year 2 (Sem IV)** | Diploma in Discipline | 80 Credits + 4 Internship Credits | Vocational Skill Certificate |
| **Year 3 (Sem VI)** | Bachelor's Degree | 120 Credits | Graduate Degree Standard |
| **Year 4 (Sem VIII)**| Bachelor's Degree (Hons / Research) | 160 Credits (with Thesis) | Honours / Research Scholar |

---

## 4. Software Enforcement Rules in ERP
1. **Credit Basket Validation**: Dynamic validation during enrollment ensures credit distributions fulfill NEP ratios before approving registration.
2. **Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) ID**: Syncs student earned credit tokens directly with National Academic Depository (NAD) / Digilocker integration endpoints.
