# Admissions & Intake Workflow Specification

## 1. Admission Lifecycle Stages
1. **Enquiry & Registration**: Applicant registers personal details & entrance exam rankings.
2. **Document Verification**: Application document verification by admissions officers.
3. **Cutoff & Merit Allotment**: Automated merit list ranking based on entrance scores.
4. **Seat Allotment & Provisional Admission**: Seat offer dispatch to candidate.
5. **Fee Payment & Roll Generation**: Admission fee payment confirmation and automatic generation of Roll Number (`2026CS108`) and Registration Number (`REG-2026-89012`).
6. **Enrollment**: Final student account activation.
