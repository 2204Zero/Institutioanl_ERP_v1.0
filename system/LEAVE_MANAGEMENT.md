# Institutional ERP Suite — Leave Management & Approval Workflows

## 1. Supported Leave Categories
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 8 days annual entitlement.
- **Medical Leave (ML)**: 10 days entitlement with medical cert upload.
- **Earned Leave (EL)**: Accrued at 2.5 days per completed month.
- **Study / Sabbatical Leave**: Granted for research fellowships and post-doctoral work.

---

## 2. Approval Routing
1. Employee submits leave request with date range and justification.
2. Head of Department (HOD) receives notification and reviews schedule impact.
3. Upon approval, leave balance is debited automatically.
