# Institutional ERP Suite — Employee Lifecycle & Digital Profile Management

## 1. Lifecycle States & Transitions
Supported employment lifecycle states:
- `JOINING` $\rightarrow$ `PROBATION` $\rightarrow$ `CONFIRMED`
- `TRANSFERRED` / `PROMOTED`
- `SUSPENDED` / `RESIGNED` / `RETIRED` / `TERMINATED`
- `ALUMNI_FACULTY` (Retained in institutional research index)

---

## 2. Categories & Pay Scales
- **Faculty**: Professors, Associate Professors, Assistant Professors (7th Pay Levels 10 to 14).
- **Non-Teaching Staff**: Technical Officers, Accounts Officers, Registrar Office.
- **Contract & Visiting Faculty**: Ad-hoc semester lecturers.

---

## 3. Digital Employee File & Document Repository
Every employee record links to verified document scans:
- Ph.D. & Degree Certificates
- PAN & Aadhaar Validation
- Bank Account Details & IFSC Code
- Emergency Contact Matrix
