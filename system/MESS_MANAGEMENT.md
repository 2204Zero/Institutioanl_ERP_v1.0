# Mess Management, Meal Plans, & Food Inventory

## 1. Meal Plans & Daily Menus
- **Boarding Plans**: Full Board (Breakfast + Lunch + Dinner), Lunch & Dinner, or Breakfast Only.
- **Meal Coupons & Biometric Check-in**: Scans student QR code or biometric fingerprint at mess entrance counter to log meal attendance.
- **Mess Billing Sync**: Monthly mess bills auto-post to student finance ledgers in `FinanceService`.
